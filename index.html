    BEFORE YOU GET IN TOUCH

    A little clarity.
    A better start.

    What should I include in my first enquiry?

    The currencies, approximate amount, intended destination and general purpose are a useful starting point. Avoid including account passwords, full bank details or identity documents in an initial email.

    How are exchange rates and charges confirmed?

    Request a transaction-specific breakdown from the applicable provider before proceeding. The rate, provider charges and any intermediary or receiving-bank charges should be considered together. This website does not publish live quotes.

    What can affect the timing of a transfer?

    The route, currency, bank cut-off times, non-working days and any information checks can affect processing. Ask for an estimate for the specific transfer before making time-sensitive arrangements.

    Can I make a transaction on this website?

    This is an information and enquiry website. It does not accept funds or payment instructions. Service availability, the relevant provider and applicable terms must be confirmed separately.
